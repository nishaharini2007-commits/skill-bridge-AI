# SkillBridge AI — SIH demo guide

## End-to-end student → mentor → student journey (about 7 minutes)

1. Start the services with `start.bat`; open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Sign in as `student@skillbridge.demo` / `Student@123`. Arun Kumar's dashboard shows live readiness, skill gaps, roadmap activity and matched opportunities.
3. Open **Career goal** and choose **Data Analyst** if it is not already selected. The choice is saved to SQLite and drives the role requirements, gaps and roadmap.
4. Open **Assessment**, answer the 10 questions and submit. The API scores answers, updates proficiency records and refreshes the career analysis.
5. Open **Skill gap** to compare current and required skill levels, inspect the priority list and see course → practice → project recommendations.
6. Open **Learning profile** to set study time and format, record self-reported skill levels, and optionally add completed courses, projects and profile links. Self-reported skills are not marked verified.
7. Open **Skill gap analysis** to inspect the rule-based comparison, provisional-evidence notes, readiness formula and resource searches or official documentation links. The recommendations are not generated by an LLM.
8. Open **Roadmap**, change a task to In Progress or Completed, then refresh or navigate away and back. Progress is persisted separately from skill proficiency; completing a task does not verify or raise a skill.
9. Open **Industry projects**, apply to **Customer Churn Analysis**, then submit a short description and optional project URL.
10. Log out and sign in as `mentor@skillbridge.demo` / `Mentor@123`. Open **Evaluations**, score each dimension from 1–5, add feedback and save.
11. Sign back in as the student. The **Skill passport** now shows the evaluation, evidence and verified project skills. **Opportunities** displays refreshed proficiency-based matches.

## Industry workflow (about 2 minutes)

1. Sign in as `industry@skillbridge.demo` / `Industry@123`.
2. Open **JD analyzer** and analyze a description containing Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel and statistics.
3. Open **Projects**, post a project with required skills and minimum levels, eligibility, deadline and evaluation criteria, then publish it.
4. Open **Job vacancies** to post and manage roles with location, employment type, required skill levels, eligibility and deadline.
5. Open **Review applicants**, select a project or vacancy, and inspect each candidate's recorded skill evidence and skills to improve. Use the status selector and company note to review, shortlist or invite a student. Match information is advisory; company users make all decisions.
6. **Skill requirements** updates role proficiency requirements that feed into student gap calculations.

## Student industry opportunities

1. Sign in as a student and open **Industry projects** or **Job vacancies**.
2. Review requirements, eligibility, deadlines and the explainable comparison to recorded skills. Apply to projects or vacancies; applications are not blocked by a low match.
3. Open **Applications** to track company status updates and notes. Project submissions and mentor evaluation continue through the existing project workflow.

## Institution view (about 1 minute)

Sign in as `institution@skillbridge.demo` / `Institution@123` and open the dashboard. Readiness, assessment participation, skill gaps, industry demand and project participation are computed from database records.
